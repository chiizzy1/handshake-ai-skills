# Substantive evaluation coverage

For new September 5 tasks, there is no per-file ask quota. The rubric must still
reach 25+ criteria, supported by meaningful analytical content. The announcement
does not prescribe a new weighting scheme.

Express the connected business need in natural prose. Useful coverage may
include a comparison, explanation of a material driver, uncertainty, feasibility,
decision sensitivity, or a visual that makes evidence easier to assess.
Include only what the stakeholder actually needs.

Do not count the same finding multiple times because it appears in a chart and
a table. Do not manufacture criteria from typography, exact phrasing or arbitrary
precision. Do not add unrelated decisions simply to reach a count.

Specify legitimate scope and constraints. Method-selection difficulty never
justifies withholding information needed to determine a correct answer.
