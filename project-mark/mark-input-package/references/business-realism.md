# Business realism

The September 5 staff announcement permits LLM help and requires authors to
iterate and edit golden deliverables until they look like real work products.

Use the format and amount of detail the intended user needs. Review factual
accuracy, useful tables/charts, readable layout, meaningful labels and an
actionable determination. Avoid generic filler and unnecessary template blocks.

Do not fabricate author names, company provenance, timestamps, approval stamps
or source documents. Authored scenario material should be identified as such.
Default library metadata can be cleaned or replaced with truthful project
metadata; it is not proof of poor analytical quality by itself.

Generate empirical figures from the source rather than hand-typing them. Render
or open the finished files and inspect their appearance. A style linter flags
possible issues; it cannot establish authenticity, correct analysis or platform
approval.
