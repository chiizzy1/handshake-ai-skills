# Fair analytical challenges

Read the [operative rules](../../shared-references/canonical-rules.md) first.
Difficulty must come from a consequential analytical mistake under a clear,
answerable task contract.

| Check | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| Answerability | Necessary data, definitions, scope, thresholds and conflict-resolution rules are supplied. |
| Reproducibility | Independent analysts can reconstruct the decisive results from the frozen inputs. |
| Determinacy | The decision is stable across valid methods and reasonable alternatives, or the task specifies a defensible decision rule. |
| Materiality | A plausible shortcut changes a substantive result, recommendation, feasibility or justification. |
| Honest provenance | Empirical values and source claims are accurate; authored assumptions and transformations are identified. |

LLM assistance with scenario documents, analysis and reviewed deliverables is
allowed. Never invent observations, people, approvals, collection timestamps or
audit history and present them as empirical evidence. Business realism means
credible, useful work with accurate provenance.

A valid challenge may involve a wrong denominator, an invalid join, temporal
leakage, conflicting source definitions, dependent constraints or an unsuitable
statistical interpretation. It does not require a deceptive summary, buried
rule, awkward file format or injected corruption. Necessary evidence must be
findable with normal tools.

Specify the business question and relevant assumptions without prescribing every
analytical step. Do not withhold a required definition merely because explaining
it might make the task easier. If equally defensible interpretations produce
different decisions, repair the contract before evaluating models.

Compare the correct analysis with plausible shortcuts and test reasonable
alternatives. A formatting penalty, valid alternate confidence interval or
cosmetic tie-break is not a substantive failure. A wrong supporting analysis
can matter even when the final recommendation happens to be correct.

Shortcut experiments show a possible mechanism, not an observed model failure.
There is no established rule that each additional trap halves the pass rate.
Use fresh blind solves, followed by the version-matched official evaluation,
to measure difficulty. Two clean local solves are a reason to retire or
materially redesign the concept; that is a local research rule, not a platform
requirement.
