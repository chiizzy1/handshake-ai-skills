# Composing analytical requirements

Link requirements only when they belong to the same decision and their computed
results interact. No fixed gate/flip/confirmation recipe or number of layers is
mandatory. Earlier model-catch percentages were not validated for our current
agents.

For each proposed requirement record:

- the real evidence and the supported analytical treatment;
- a plausible incorrect treatment and its observable result;
- which decision or material supporting finding changes;
- what reasonable alternatives were tested.

Examples to investigate include assignment cohorts combined with follow-up
selection, exposure combined with outcome timing, or decision-time data vintages
combined with forecast validation and operational constraints. These are
hypotheses to test, not guaranteed traps.

Do not deepen a task merely by moving a definition into another file. Preserve
discoverability and complete scope. Contradictory definitions or fabricated
numbers are defects. A task that stays difficult when evidence is organized
clearly provides stronger support for an analytical challenge.

Prototype before full packaging. Record blind solver outcomes separately from
self-checks and official platform evaluation. A correct final choice with
materially wrong supporting analysis can still reveal a real failure; do not
discard or invent findings just to force a different winner.
